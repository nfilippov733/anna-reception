## The standout lever: the 14-day free trial

"Try free for 14 days — no card, cancel anytime" is the single biggest change.
It converts every persona's residual hesitation ("is it worth it / will it work
for me / am I locked in?") into "I'll just try it." It compounds with the new
tariff names that let each buyer *see themselves* in a plan.

## ANNA-style tariff names = instant self-ID

- **Sole Trader** (£99) — names the one-person operator outright.
- **Business** (£179, recommended, trial) — busy single site.
- **Big Business** (£299) — multi-site / chains.

Plain, confident, ANNA-Money cadence ("Business / Big Business"). A sole-trader
plumber reads "Sole Trader" and thinks *that's me* — far stronger than "Solo".
