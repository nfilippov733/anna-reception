## What still leaks (honest)

These are no longer *copy/positioning* problems — they're **proof and product**:

1. **All-illustrative credibility.** Testimonials "Illustrative", stats
   "estimate, 2025", synthetic demo audio, no logos/customer counts. This is the
   single remaining blocker for the sceptics (Sarah won't sign, Macleod won't
   commit) and it can't be fixed with copy — it needs **1–2 real references per
   vertical** (or radically honest "early-access / pilot" framing).
2. **Integration reliability / per-PMS proof.** The complements block now leans on
   "books into your system" — which invites "does it reliably write to *my*
   Dentally/Fresha, no double-bookings?" Needs per-PMS "verified" proof, not a
   logo wall.
3. **Everything still funnels to a demo.** Trial, pricing, accuracy ("hear your
   hardest calls on the demo") all route to Book a demo. Honest now, but a
   self-serve buyer still can't try it alone. A genuine self-serve trial would be
   the next leverage step — product, not page.
4. **Vet-specific:** formal liability/indemnity wording, not just "you stay in
   control". A regulated buyer wants the contract position.

## Bottom line
The new blocks did their job: the *positioning* leaks are closed and 5 of 6
personas now lean toward a trial/demo, with James a clear convert. What's left
isn't writing — it's **real proof** (references, verified integrations) and a
**true self-serve trial**. Those are the next moves, and they're product/data
decisions, not landing-page copy.
