# Critical persona pass — competitor-aware, final funnel

**Run:** 2026-06-01 · main at `2ed5242` (live)
**Brief:** previous passes were too kind — everyone converted. This run uses
sceptical, competitor-aware buyers who already use a booking system and have
seen other "AI receptionist" pitches. Honest verdicts, including the ones who
*don't* convert and where the funnel leaks.

---

## The funnel as it actually is

Hero → calculators (leak + vs-alternatives) → channel demos (voice + WhatsApp) →
segments carousel → pricing (Sole Trader / Business / Big Business, +VAT,
14-day trial) → FAQ. **Every conversion path ends at "Book a demo"** — the
trial CTA is "Book a demo and start a trial", the audit verification books a
demo, pricing books a demo. There is no self-serve sign-up or instant trial.

That's the first thing a sceptic notices: **it's sales-led, not self-serve.**

## Per-persona (critical)

### 🦷 Dr. Sarah K. — Dental
- **Competitor in her head:** "Dentally already has online booking and SMS
  reminders. Smith.ai exists. Why add another layer?"
- **Objections the page doesn't answer:** no real dental customer (testimonials
  say "Illustrative"), stats are "industry estimate, 2025", the call audio is a
  synthetic voice — she wants to hear ANNA handle a *real* nervous patient and
  triage. No "how is this different from my PMS's own tools".
- **Verdict:** books a demo *to interrogate it* — not a soft conversion. Won't
  commit without a real dental reference + hearing live triage. **Qualified, not won.**

### 💇 Charlie R. — Beauty
- **Competitor:** "My clients already book on **Fresha**, and it sends reminders
  and takes deposits. I get WhatsApps, sure — but is ANNA worth it on top of Fresha?"
- **Gap:** the page never rebuts "my booking app already does most of this." The
  WhatsApp demo is strong, but she's not sure it's incremental vs free Fresha tools.
- **Verdict:** **Lukewarm.** Likes the WhatsApp angle; "I already have Fresha" is
  unaddressed. Would only trial if it's clearly additive.

### 🍺 Emma & Joe — Gastropub
- **Competitor:** "**OpenTable / SevenRooms** take our covers online already, and
  we've seen **Slang.ai** for restaurant phones."
- **Objection:** another tool to manage; allergen/deposit handling is nice but
  "does it beat what OpenTable already does on the phone?" isn't shown.
- **Verdict:** **Conditional** — trial only if it visibly out-does their res system.

### 🔧 James W. — Trades
- Still the warmest (he genuinely loses jobs on the roof), but now sharper:
  "Every AI-answering startup says this. Will it actually understand a panicked
  customer with a thick Geordie accent? What if it books the *wrong* job or quotes
  a price I can't honour?" Aware mates use a cheap answering service already.
- **Friction:** "14-day free trial" but the button says *Book a demo* — "I don't
  want a sales call, I want to try it tonight." The sales gate cools him.
- **Verdict:** **books a demo, warmest of the six** — but with real accuracy doubts
  and mild irritation at the demo gate.

### 🏋 Maya R. — Fitness
- **Competitor:** "**Mindbody / TeamUp** already do booking, waitlists and
  reminders. What does ANNA add that my platform doesn't?"
- **Gap:** class-fill ROI still not surfaced; differentiation vs her platform absent.
- **Verdict:** **Not convinced** ANNA beats Mindbody's native tooling for the price.

### 🐾 Dr. Macleod — Vet
- Highest bar. "An AI mishandling triage is a welfare and **liability** issue.
  Show me it's RCVS-aware, who's liable if it gets triage wrong, and a *real* vet
  practice using it." Knows telehealth/triage players exist.
- **Dealbreaker:** every testimonial is "Illustrative"; the triage demo is synthetic.
  He won't risk animal welfare on mocked proof.
- **Verdict:** **Does not convert.** "Come back when you have a vet reference and a
  liability answer." A cautious demo at most.

## Where the funnel leaks (and how to fix)

1. **No competitor / "I already have a booking system" rebuttal.** The single
   biggest leak: Charlie/Maya/Emma all bounce on "my platform already does this."
   → Add a short "ANNA vs your booking system / vs other AI answering" angle:
   ANNA *answers the live call/DM and books into* that system — it's the front
   desk, not another booking page. Reframe as complementary, not replacement.
2. **Everything is illustrative.** Testimonials "Illustrative", stats "estimate",
   synthetic demo audio, no logos/counts. Sceptics (Sarah, Macleod) stall.
   → Get 1–2 real references per vertical, or be radically honest ("pilot
   pricing, early access") rather than mock-confident.
3. **Demo-gated trial.** "14-day free trial" + button "Book a demo" reads as
   sales-led, not a real trial. James/Charlie want to just try it.
   → Either a genuine self-serve trial, or rename to set expectations
   ("Book a 15-min setup call — then 14 days free") so it doesn't feel bait-y.
4. **No AI-accuracy / failure-mode reassurance.** James/Macleod worry about
   mishearing, wrong bookings, accents, liability. FAQ covers "can't answer →
   transfer" but not "gets it wrong".
   → Add accuracy/edge-case + liability FAQ; let people hear a hard call
   (accent, flustered caller), not just a clean one.
5. **Integration depth is a logo wall, not proof.** "Works where supported" —
   buyers want proof it writes to *their* exact PMS.
   → Per-PMS "verified" badges or a short "how the Dentally/Fresha write-back
   works" note.

## Bottom line
The page is *beautifully* built and the value prop is clear — but against
competitor-aware, sceptical buyers it leaks on (a) differentiation vs their
existing booking system, (b) all-illustrative proof, and (c) a trial that's
actually a sales call. Closing #1 and #3 would move Charlie/Emma/Maya from
"maybe/no" toward "trial", and real proof (#2) is the only thing that unblocks
Macleod.
